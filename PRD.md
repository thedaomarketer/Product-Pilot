# Product Requirements Document

## Product

ProductPilot AI

## Version

MVP v1.0

## Product Category

E-commerce Product Intelligence SaaS

---

# 1. Problem

E-commerce sellers struggle to determine:

- What products to sell
- Whether demand exists
- How competitive a market is
- What price to charge
- Whether a product can be profitable
- How to differentiate
- How to create optimized listings

Existing tools often focus on isolated functions.

ProductPilot combines research, opportunity evaluation, profitability, and listing creation into one workflow.

---

# 2. Target User

Primary:

Independent Etsy seller.

Secondary:

- Shopify seller
- Amazon seller
- Print-on-demand seller
- 3D-printing seller
- Handmade business
- Small DTC brand
- E-commerce agency

---

# 3. Core Value Proposition

ProductPilot helps sellers decide:

"What should I sell, and is it worth pursuing?"

---

# 4. MVP FEATURES

## Product Research

User enters:

- Product
- Niche
- Marketplace
- Target customer
- Budget
- Production method
- Location
- Business model

System creates an analysis.

---

## Opportunity Score

Calculate a 0-100 score using defined scoring methodology.

Display:

- Overall score
- Demand
- Competition
- Margin
- Trend
- Differentiation
- Risk
- Confidence

---

## Profit Calculator

Inputs:

- Selling price
- Product cost
- Shipping
- Packaging
- Marketplace fees
- Payment fees
- Advertising
- Labor
- Other costs

Outputs:

- Gross profit
- Net profit
- Margin
- Break-even price
- Break-even orders
- Profit projections

---

## Listing Generator

Generate:

- Title
- Description
- Tags
- Keywords
- Materials
- Attributes
- FAQs
- Image concepts
- Alt text

---

## Product Saving

Users can save:

- Product
- Analysis
- Score
- Listing
- Profit assumptions

---

## Dashboard

Display:

- Recent analyses
- Saved products
- Usage
- Subscription
- Quick actions

---

# 5. NON-GOALS

MVP does not include:

- Automated marketplace publishing
- Full competitor monitoring
- Automated sales forecasting
- Multi-user teams
- Agency dashboards
- Automated advertising
- Inventory management
- Order management

---

# 6. SUCCESS METRICS

Primary:

- Account activation
- Product analysis completion
- Listing generation
- Paid conversion
- Retention
- Monthly recurring revenue

Secondary:

- Analyses per user
- Saved opportunities
- Profit calculations
- Return visits
- Listing regeneration

---

# 7. ACTIVATION EVENT

A user becomes activated when they:

1. Create an account.
2. Complete one product analysis.
3. View the opportunity score.
4. Generate at least one listing.

---

# 8. MVP ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

A new user must be able to complete the core workflow without assistance.

The complete workflow:

Sign up
→ Dashboard
→ Analyze product
→ View report
→ Calculate profit
→ Generate listing
→ Save
→ Return later
